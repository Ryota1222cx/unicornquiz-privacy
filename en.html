Unicorn Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Information we collect

Unicorn Quiz does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or location. The app shows no ads and includes no analytics tools.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, points, study streak, and reminder time are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer's servers. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time course purchases are processed by Google Play. The app and its developer do not receive your payment details. The app uses Google Play Billing to check what you have purchased.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled and shown by your device. You can change the notification permission in your device settings at any time.

Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
